This tool is especially useful to overcome scenic obstacles. You can shoot quick while jumping or aim while standing. The arrows gets stuck almost anywhere, including vehicles or living creatures. If the SFT is on the rope, he can swing with [Left/Right] and control the pulley with [Up/Down]. Therefor you don't need to keep the Harpoon in the first place of the inventory. You can cut the rope with [Throw]. There is a second mode that shoots explosive arrows. Activate it with [Special2]. You need the Grenade Box as ammunition.
